Questions brands ask first

How much budget do I need?

Campaigns can start small with a handful of micro creators. We will suggest a plan that fits your budget and goal after the brief.

How do you choose creators?

We check audience fit, engagement quality, past brand work and content style, then you approve the shortlist.

How long does a campaign take?

Most campaigns go live within two to three weeks of the brief, depending on approvals and product shipping.

Do we get the rights to the content?

Yes. Usage rights are agreed in advance, including paid ads, so you know exactly where content can run.

What reporting do I get?

A simple report with reach, engagement, clicks and sales impact, plus what we recommend doing next.
